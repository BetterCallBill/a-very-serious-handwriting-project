## Interview questions and answers / 面试问答

### Q: Does adding obj to .gitignore remove files already tracked by Git? / 将 obj 加入 .gitignore 会移除已跟踪文件吗？

**Answer:** No. Ignore rules prevent matching untracked files from being added normally. They do not remove existing index entries or change previous commits. Stopping future tracking and removing files from historical commits are separate tasks.

**参考回答：** 不会。忽略规则防止匹配的未跟踪文件被正常添加，但不会移除已有索引条目，也不会修改旧提交。停止今后的跟踪与清理历史提交是不同任务。

### Q: How do the working tree, index, and commit history differ during cleanup? / 清理时工作区、索引与提交历史有什么区别？

**Answer:** The working tree contains files on disk. The index represents the snapshot prepared for a commit. Commit history contains previously recorded snapshots. We can remove generated files from tracking while preserving local copies; removing them from earlier commits requires rewriting those snapshots as well. Deleting local files alone does not remove their historical versions.

**参考回答：** 工作区是磁盘上的文件，索引是为提交准备的快照，提交历史保存过去记录的快照。可以停止跟踪生成文件，同时保留本地副本；要从旧提交中移除，还必须重写历史快照。只删除本地文件不能删除历史版本。

### Q: How would you verify that history cleanup preserved unrelated work? / 如何验证历史清理没有影响其他工作？

**Answer:** Compare working-file fingerprints before and after, compare unrelated file blobs at the old and rewritten tips, inspect the index and rewritten commits for remaining target paths, and confirm the ignore rules match the intended directories. The session recorded unchanged contents for all 71 existing tracked and untracked working paths, identical non-target blobs at the tip, no remaining `obj` paths in the checked index/history, and matching ignore rules for both project directories.

**参考回答：** 对比清理前后的工作文件指纹、旧分支顶端与重写后顶端的无关文件 blob，检查索引和重写提交中是否仍有目标路径，并确认忽略规则匹配目标目录。当日记录确认 71 个已有工作路径内容不变、顶端非目标文件 blob 一致、检查范围内没有残留 obj 路径，且两个项目目录均匹配忽略规则。

### Q: Why does rewriting history affect pushing, and what does a lease protect? / 为什么重写历史会影响推送，lease 保护什么？

**Answer:** Rewriting commits changes their IDs, so the cleaned branch generally cannot replace the published branch through a normal fast-forward push. A force push with a lease checks the remote ref against an expected value before replacing it, protecting against unexpected remote changes. It still rewrites shared history when that check passes. No such push was performed in this session.

**参考回答：** 重写提交会改变提交 ID，因此通常不能通过普通快进推送替换远程分支。带 lease 的强制推送会先检查远程引用是否符合预期值，防止覆盖意外的远程变化。检查通过后仍然会重写共享历史；当日没有执行这种推送。

### Q: Does a cleaned local branch mean the old objects have been securely erased? / 本地分支清理后，旧对象是否已被安全擦除？

**Answer:** No. Remote-tracking refs, recovery refs, reflogs, and backups can retain the original history. The session recorded a recovery bundle at `/private/tmp/eventbooking-git-cleanup.EJ7cvy/before.bundle`, with an index backup, fingerprints, and commit mappings alongside it. That is a historical recovery location, not a guarantee the temporary files still exist; the operating system may remove them.

**参考回答：** 不代表。远程跟踪引用、恢复引用、reflog 和备份仍可能保留旧历史。当日记录了 `/private/tmp/eventbooking-git-cleanup.EJ7cvy/before.bundle` 恢复包，同目录还有索引备份、文件指纹和提交映射。这是历史恢复位置，不保证临时文件目前仍存在；操作系统可能清除它们。
